# Interim Autonomy Resolution Reconciliation

Status: **INTERIM / RECONCILIATION REQUIRED**

## Purpose

Record small pre-Mind-Engine autonomy-resolution layers that temporarily improve ordinary behavior without creating a competing planning system.

These layers are allowed only when they remain character-generic, soft, reversible, and subordinate to deterministic action authority.

## Current interim layer: soft multi-hop purpose continuity

The universal autonomy policy may treat a legal intermediate move as useful when represented spatial knowledge shows that it advances toward a contextually worthwhile known destination or activity.

This is intentionally limited:

- no character-specific destination rule;
- no outdoor quota or schedule;
- no elapsed-time trigger such as "leave after N hours";
- no hidden score or forced destination preference;
- no persistent goal, intention, route plan, or planner state;
- no topology/access authority outside current deterministic `action_options`;
- the character reassesses at every normal decision boundary.

The layer exists because current cognition already receives actor-known spatial connections, familiar lifestyle destinations, world affordances, recent history, profile/state context, and the exact legal action surface, but MIND-F0 is still behavior-neutral and persistent intention/planning is not active.

## Mandatory future reconciliation

When Intelligent Mind Engine intention/planning runtime is implemented, this interim guidance must be reviewed rather than silently retained.

Expected migration direction:

`represented opportunity/perception + recall + active mental state -> intention_candidate / plan_candidate -> legal route-aware action proposal -> deterministic action authority`

At that point:

1. decide which behavior belongs in a typed Mind artifact or planning socket;
2. migrate durable route purpose into the Mind Engine where appropriate;
3. remove or reduce duplicate prompt-level guidance so two planning authorities do not coexist;
4. preserve deterministic topology/access/action validation as final authority;
5. rerun natural-behavior acceptance for indoor/outdoor transitions and broader multi-hop destination use.

## Reconciliation rule for future minimum layers

Any new temporary resolution layer added before its complete canonical subsystem exists must be recorded here or in a successor reconciliation registry with:

- the observed gap it addresses;
- why the full subsystem is deferred;
- its authority boundary;
- what it must migrate into later;
- the condition that triggers retirement/reconciliation.

Minimum layers are scaffolding, not permanent parallel architecture.
